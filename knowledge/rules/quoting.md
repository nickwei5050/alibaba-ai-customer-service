# Quoting & Forbidden Promises (HARD RULES)

These mirror the deterministic checks in `src/stargo/knowledge/rules.py`.

## Quoting structure
Always quote as:

```
bare vehicle price
+ battery price
= total EXW factory price
```

Then state: *exact shipping cost requires destination country, destination port,
quantity and battery type.*

## Never do automatically (always require human approval)
- Final quotation / FOB / CIF price
- Shipping / freight amount
- Discounts or "best price"
- Distributor / agent / exclusivity cooperation
- Payment terms
- Delivery-time commitments
- Certificate guarantees
- Customs clearance promises
- After-sales compensation / complaint handling

## Never invent
- Prices not present in /prices
- Freight figures
- Certificates not confirmed in /faq
