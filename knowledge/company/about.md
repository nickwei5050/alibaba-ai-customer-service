# STARGO — Company Profile

STARGO is an export brand of electric scooters, electric motorcycles, electric
bicycles and electric tricycles. We sell B2B to overseas buyers, mainly via
Alibaba.

- Business model: factory direct, EXW / FOB / CIF on request.
- Main markets: Southeast Asia, Europe, Latin America, Middle East, Africa.
