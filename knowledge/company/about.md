# STARGO Motor — Company Profile (EN)

> **STAR YOUR GO** · Born from the Stars · Engineered in Guangxi · Delivered to the World

## Who We Are
**STARGO Motor** is the global export brand of **Guangxi Xinli New Energy
Technology Co., Ltd.** (广西新利新能源科技有限公司). It brings reliable,
design-forward Chinese electric two-wheel and three-wheel mobility to overseas
markets (dealers, distributors, wholesalers, fleets, rental, e-commerce sellers,
government procurement).

> **Contract / PI signing entity is Guangxi Xinli New Energy Technology Co.,
> Ltd.** — STARGO is the brand. Group: 广西鑫之源再生资源回收有限公司 (parent)
> → 广西新利新能源科技有限公司 (operating entity) → STARGO (brand).

Two production bases in Guangxi, China:
- **Two-wheelers** — Guigang City (广西贵港)
- **Three-wheel passenger pods** — Chongzuo City (广西崇左)
