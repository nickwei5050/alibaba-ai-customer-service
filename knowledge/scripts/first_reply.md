# Sales Scripts — First Reply (information collection)

Use this tone for a first contact when the buyer only said "Hi" or "price".

> Hi {name}, thank you for your interest in STARGO electric vehicles.
> To prepare an accurate quotation, could you please confirm:
> 1. Model you are interested in
> 2. Order quantity
> 3. Preferred battery option (lead-acid or lithium)
> 4. Destination country and port
> 5. Whether you need EXW, FOB or CIF pricing?

Keep it warm, short, and focused on collecting the five fields above.
