# 询盘回复 SOP / Inquiry Reply Playbook

13 个核心场景模板。AI 优先用于**收集信息 + 推荐车型**;任何报价转人工。

## 5 大原则
1. **24-Hour Rule** — 任何询盘 24h 内回应
2. **Qualify First** — 报价前先问:数量、目的地、用途
3. **Never Send Just a Number** — 价格必须配卖点 + 下一步(但本系统价格一律转人工)
4. **WhatsApp First** — 东南亚/非洲/中东/拉美尽早引导到 WhatsApp
5. **Keep It Short** — 3–5 小段优于一大段

## 标准流程
5 分钟内 acknowledgement → 判断客户类型(纯询价/技术型/经销商)→ 不直接给最低价 →
先问数量/目的地/配置/用途 → 推荐车型 → 引导 WhatsApp → 报价后 24h/3 日/7 日三轮跟进。

## 场景模板(英文)

### 1. 纯询价型 — 先资格审查
```
Hi [Name],
To prepare a competitive quotation:
1. Which model(s)? (I can send the catalog)
2. Target quantity?
3. Destination port / country?
4. For resale, project, or personal use?
You'll get a quotation + factory video within 24 hours.
Nick | WhatsApp: [+86-187-7512-7878]
```

### 2. 要 Catalog
```
Attached is our 2026 STARGO catalog. For a faster recommendation:
- Which segment: commuter / off-road / cargo tricycles?
- Estimated first-order quantity?
- Destination country?
I'll send 2-3 specific recommendations within 24 hours.
```

### 3. 问 MOQ
```
Standard MOQ: 20 units/model. Mix 2-3 models: 10 units/model as long as the
total hits 20-25 units. Sample (3-5 units): +15-25% per unit due to LCL.
```

### 4. 问样品 → 见 sample/moq-and-sample.md

### 5. "价格太高"(永远不要直接降价)
```
Before I come back with options, could you share what you're comparing against?
Adjustments available: (1) adjust specs to match target price (2) volume discount
if quantity increases (3) longer payment cycle for larger orders.
```

### 6. "别家更便宜" — 用成本拆解教育(电池型号/电机瓦数/认证/包装),请对方分享竞品规格书。

### 7. "我考虑一下" — 提供 1 页决策摘要 + QC 视频 + 同区域买家参考 + 产能档期(可保留 3 个工作日)。

### 8. OEM/自有品牌 — OEM(贴标/手册/包装)MOQ 50;ODM(模具/App)MOQ 200+,交期 60–90 天。⚠️ 知识产权须律师确认。

### 9. 问交期
```
20-30 days after deposit. I can hold the production slot for 3 business days.
```
⚠️ 不要许诺比市场快的交期。

### 10. 问认证 — 已有:主动提供规格书+认证副本+工厂视频;没有:"可为您的订单去申请"+反问是否强制。

### 11. 问售后/Warranty
```
Warranty:
• Frame & motor: 24 months from B/L date
• Battery: 12 months (>=70% capacity)
• Controller/dashboard/charger: 12 months
• Wear parts (tires, brake pads): 6 months
First container: free spare-parts package (~3% of order value).
```
⚠️ 具体条款 Nick 确认。

### 12. 想做经销商 → 见 scripts/distributor.md

### 13. 报价后沉默 — 3 天:确认收到+产品视频/装柜照;7–14 天:产能紧迫感+低门槛回复。

## 附录:常用短语
- 推进成交:`Shall I issue the Proforma Invoice so we can move forward?`
- 催定金:`Once we receive the 30% deposit, we'll start production immediately.`
- 转 WhatsApp:`Just sent you an email — switching to WhatsApp in case it's easier.`

## 附录:7 大诈骗红旗
大单+零细节(80% 诈骗)· 大单要 PayPal(100% 拒绝)· LC 软条款(新买家勿碰)·
拒透露公司信息(停止对接)· 起步就催促(slow down)· 收货地址≠付款方(跨境诈骗)·
规格未定就杀价(先锁规格再谈价)。
