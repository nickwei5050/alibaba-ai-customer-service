# 经销商 / 代理话术 (Distributor & Agent)

## 原则
- 欢迎经销合作,但**先验证实力**,再谈条件。
- **独家(exclusive/sole agent)请求一律转人工**,绝不自动答应。独家承诺须律师确认,
  通常以"试用期 + 业绩门槛 + first-right-of-refusal"为框架。

## 12A 一般经销意向 — 表达欢迎 + 反问 4 个问题验证实力
```
Great to hear you're interested in becoming a STARGO distributor. To recommend
the right partnership level, could you share:
1. Which market/country and cities do you cover?
2. Your current product lines and sales channels?
3. Expected annual volume?
4. Do you have local certification / after-sales capability?
```

## 12B 过早要独家 — 用试用期框架(不承诺)
```
We're open to exclusivity for committed partners. Typically we start with a
6-month trial period and a volume milestone, with first-right-of-refusal on
exclusivity afterward. Let's align on targets first.
```
⚠️ **独家/排他/区域保护 = 高风险,必须 Nick + 律师确认,不可自动答复。**

## 利润系数参考(内部,不对客)
客户类型 × 批量决定利润系数(零售 1.35–1.50 → 独家代理 1.15–1.20)。
**此为内部定价机制,绝不写入对客回复;具体报价转 Nick。**
